Syllabus
An outline or summary of a course's requirements, grading criteria, reading assignments, and exam dates provided by the instructor at the start of the term.
Prerequisite
A specific course or requirement that a student must successfully complete before they are allowed to enroll in a more advanced course.
GPA (Grade Point Average)
A numeric evaluation of a student's academic performance, calculated by averaging the numerical points earned across all completed courses.
FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid)
A standard form filled out annually by current and prospective college students to determine their eligibility for federal financial aid, including grants, loans, and work-study programs.
Academic Probation
A temporary status assigned to a student whose cumulative GPA falls below the university's minimum requirement, serving as a warning to improve their academic performance.
